Grid positions are schematic. The graticule is fitted to the frame so the whole country is visible. Only the displacements are drawn at the selected scale — the same bargain the scale bar makes, where the bar's position is arbitrary and its length is true.
This compares operations, not truth. Plates agreeing does not make any of them correct. Establishing that needs a control point or an independent reference, neither of which is metadata.
Real measurements. Every offset here was computed by transforming the same British National Grid graticule through each published operation on a live QGIS 4.2.2 / PROJ 9.8 install, then measuring the ellipsoidal distance from the most accurate available result. Nothing is illustrative.
Why the ballpark is different in kind. It has no published accuracy because no datum shift was applied at all — the coordinates were reprojected unchanged. It is not a worse estimate; it is the absence of one, and it lands roughly seven times further out than the spread of everything else.
